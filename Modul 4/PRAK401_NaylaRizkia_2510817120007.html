The Wanderer is a playable Anemo character in Genshin Impact.

He was once Scaramouche, the Sixth of the Eleven Fatui Harbingers, also known by his codename "The Balladeer." After erasing his previous identities from Irminsul, he became a drifter until he willingly regained his memories.

He is currently a Driyosh of the Vahumana Darshan.
